The BADERUNISSA FOUNDATION is an active, grassroots non-governmental organization (NGO) headquartered along Bannerghatta Road in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Operating under the core motto of "Serving Humanity with Compassion," the foundation drives social impact through localized humanitarian service, dialysis support, healthcare support, public advocacy, and safety initiatives.

The primary sectors where the BADERUNISSA FOUNDATION makes a distinct community impact include:

1. Critical Healthcare and Dialysis Support 
One of the foundations’ most direct, lifesaving contributions is its active support for patients suffering from end-stage chronic kidney disease.

Treatments: Recognizing the extreme financial burden of long-term medical care, the foundation acts as a financial bridge, organizing crowd-funded or subsidized life-sustaining dialysis sessions (averaging roughly ?1,700 per session) specifically targeted toward underprivileged adults and pediatric patients.

Public Awareness: The foundation runs active civic campaigns detailing the dangers of long-term or over-the-counter painkiller abuse to actively lower regional rates of preventable kidney damage.

2. Infrastructure, Road Safety, and Public Advocacy
Unlike many traditional charitable trusts, the BADERUNISSA FOUNDATION actively steps into municipal issues, utilizing local and state administrative mechanisms to correct systemic problems.

Junction Interventions: A prominent example of their civic impact includes tackling local infrastructure bottlenecks, such as the accident-prone Jayadeva 'U' Turn junction in Bengaluru. 


Institutional Coordination: The NGO works as a liaison between everyday citizens and top governance bodies. They actively host and attend "Seva Meetings" with local enforcement units like the Mico Layout Traffic Police Station to formalize the installation of necessary traffic signals. They have raised critical safety issues directly to the BBMP/GBA Commissioner and the Karnataka State Human Rights Commission (KSHRC).


3. Education, Charity, and Social Welfare
Led administratively by its Founder-President and leadership, the organization also works across a broad range of community-welfare drives:

Crisis Assistance: Providing clothes, basic food provisions, and educational support items to low-income families and children facing sudden financial distress.

Women's Safety Advocacy: Amplifying governmental emergency systems (such as the state's round-the-clock 181 Women Helpline) to ensure domestic safety and legal counseling are widely understood within local communities.

 Importance of Humanity/Charity in Islam: 
In Islamic tradition and historical biography, humanity was of paramount importance to the holy Prophet Muhammad ? (peace be upon him). His teachings and actions consistently emphasized universal human dignity, empathy, and the preservation of life, transcending tribal, racial, and social divides.
1. Theological Purpose: A Mercy to All Creation:
In Islamic theology, the holy Prophet’s entire mission is explicitly defined by his relationship to humanity. The Quran states:"And We have not sent you, [O Muhammad], except as a mercy to the worlds." (Quran 21:107)
Islamic scholars emphasize that this "mercy" (Rahmah) is not exclusive to Muslims, but extends to all of humanity, animals, and the environment.

2. Affirmation of Universal Human Dignity:
Long before the formulation of modern human rights, the holy Prophet Muhammad ? (peace be upon him), championed the intrinsic value of human life regardless of faith, race, or status:
Abolishing Racial Supremacy: During his Farewell Sermon, he delivered a definitive statement against racism: "An Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab, nor a non-Arab has any superiority over an Arab; also, a white has no superiority over a black, nor a black has any superiority over white except by piety and good action."

3. Protection of Human Life and Rights:
The holy Prophet Muhammad ? (peace be upon him), introduced strict ethical boundaries to protect human life and societal welfare:
Sanctity of Life: He reinforced the Quranic principle that taking a single innocent life is akin to killing all of humanity (Quran 5:32).
Ethics of Engagement: Even during times of conflict, the holy Prophet Muhammad ? (peace be upon him) established strict rules of engagement that prohibited the harming of non-combatants, including women, children and the elderly, as well as the destruction of property and nature.
Social Justice: The holy Prophet Muhammad ? (peace be upon him) elevated the status of marginalized groups in 7th-century Arabia by explicitly mandating the fair treatment, financial support, and protection of orphans, widows, the poor, and the enslaved. 

4. Personal Empathy and Compassion: 
The holy Prophet Muhammad ? (peace be upon him) Biographies (Seerah) highlight his deeply compassionate nature toward everyday human suffering. He was known to shorten congregational prayers if he heard a baby crying, out of empathy for the mother's distress. He famously stated: "The Merciful One shows mercy to those who are merciful. Show mercy to those on earth, and the One in heaven will show mercy to you" (Sunan Abi Dawud).
